Privacy Policy

FLUX · Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller is the developer of the FLUX application. For any request regarding personal data, contact: flux.support.app@gmail.com

2. Data Collected

The app collects and processes the following data:

  • Identification data: first name, last name, email address;
  • Usage data: trading operations entered by the user (pair, direction, P&L, dates, times), goals and personalised settings.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data is processed exclusively to:

  • provide and manage the trade journal service;
  • allow access to the account;
  • calculate statistics and forecasts requested by the user.

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on the user's explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), provided at the time of account creation.

5. Data Retention

All data is stored exclusively on the user's device (local storage). No data is transmitted to external servers. Data is deleted upon app uninstallation or through the "Delete all trades" function in settings.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

Personal data is not shared, sold or disclosed to third parties for any purpose.

7. User Rights (GDPR)

Under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR, the user has the right to:

  • Access: obtain information about their data;
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data;
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of their data;
  • Portability: receive their data in a structured format;
  • Objection: object to processing;
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise these rights: flux.support.app@gmail.com

8. Minimum Age

The service is reserved for persons aged at least 16 (Art. 8 GDPR – Italy). Use by persons under 16 without parental or guardian consent is not permitted.

9. Changes to this Policy

This policy may be updated. Users will be notified through the app of any significant changes.

10. Supervisory Authority

The user has the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).
